3. Methodology & Product Design Tributes (Clean-Room Independent Implementation)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Inkloom pays tribute to Niklas Luhmann's Zettelkasten method, Andrej Karpathy's
LLM Wiki compilation paradigm, Piotr Wozniak's SuperMemo SM-2 algorithm, Anki,
Obsidian (JSON Canvas 1.0 & Dataview query inspiration), Notion, and Logseq.
All Inkloom source code is independently implemented without copying proprietary
or copyleft-restricted source code.
